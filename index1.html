Midnight Cafe: Mystery Library

Welcome to the Midnight Cafe!

A place with great vibes, clean space, warm atmosphere, and an open atmosphere. Every guest is welcomed here.

Our address is 8392 Go Street 11885 Ocean City, NJ. We serve breakfast items all day. Lunch items are available from 10:30 AM - 2:30 PM.

Additionally, we make freshly brewed coffee.

For Sunday Brunch we have a Sunday Funday Special! It is a delightful combination of fresh ingredients and our signature flavors. For all entrees you get an extra side for free as well as a free drink.

So what are you waiting for? Order something now and enjoy a delicious meal!
